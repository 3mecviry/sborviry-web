Support us

Your support makes a difference

The running of the church, our evangelism in the town and our help for people in need are all funded by voluntary gifts.

Why give

Where your gifts go

  • the running and upkeep of the premises where we meet;
  • evangelism in the town centre — materials and printing;
  • recording and publishing sermons and conferences;
  • practical help for people in difficult circumstances;
  • support for mission and charitable work.

No amount is too small, and nobody here is judged by how much they give. Giving is entirely voluntary.
